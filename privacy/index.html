Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026. Best Movers Denver is operated by Eye To Ad Media, Denver, Colorado.

Information collected

The fields you submit: name, phone, optional email, kind of move, ZIPs or destination, home size and preferred date. Alongside, we log the time, the page, the terms version and the exact wording of the authorization you accepted, to show consent later.

Use

To review your request, forward it to up to three movers that passed our screening, and contact you about it. To fight spam and keep legal records. We do not sell it to data brokers and we run no ad trackers here.

Sharing

Only with the movers receiving your request and with service providers that help us operate. Movers are separate companies with their own practices, and we ask them to use your details only for the move.

Contact by phone and text

Applies only if you checked the authorization box. Automated dialing or prerecorded voice may be used. STOP ends texts.

Retention

Up to 24 months, then deletion. Earlier on request.

Your choices

Email zach@eyetoad.com to view, correct or erase your information. We handle requests from everyone, in Colorado or not.

Protection

Submissions use HTTPS. Personal details never appear in URLs.

Records of consent

Each submission keeps a snapshot of the authorization sentence on screen, the time it was accepted and the version of our terms. That snapshot is what we would show a regulator or a complaining customer.

Scripts, fonts and trackers

Pages load only files from this site, plus the form post to our lead server. There are no third-party fonts, widgets or ad scripts.

Links to other sites

We link to government and nonprofit pages for sourcing. Their privacy practices belong to them.

Questions or complaints

Write to zach@eyetoad.com and we will answer in plain language.

Minors

Intended for adults. We do not knowingly gather data on anyone under 18.

Best Movers Denver is a free referral service operated by Eye To Ad Media, Denver, Colorado; not a moving company.
