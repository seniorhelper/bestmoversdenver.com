Straight answers

What does Best Movers Denver check before a mover receives a request?

Three things, every time: an active Colorado PUC household-goods permit (plus USDOT and MC numbers if the move crosses a state line), insurance in force, and a Better Business Bureau A+ rating. We look each one up ourselves.

Do you publish a ranked list of the best movers?

No. Rankings and star scores go out of date and are easy to game. We publish the standard so you can test any company, including ones we have never heard of.

Does a BBB A+ mean a mover is good?

Not by itself. BBB says its letter grade comes from factors such as complaint history, time in business, transparency, licensing and advertising problems. Customer reviews are not part of the calculation. It is one useful signal among several.

What insurance should a Colorado mover carry?

A compliance publisher's summary of the PUC rules lists 750,000 dollars combined single limit for vehicles of 10,000 pounds GVWR or more, 300,000 for lighter vehicles, and 500,000 general liability for household goods movers. Confirm with the PUC.
