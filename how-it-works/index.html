How matching works

The whole process, without marketing gloss.

  1. Step 1

    You submit the form

    Name and phone are required. Everything else just helps movers quote.

  2. Step 2

    A person reviews it

    We discard spam and requests that are not real moves.

  3. Step 3

    Only screened movers get it

    Each of up to three companies has cleared the gates on the standard.

  4. Step 4

    You hear from them

    They contact you by phone, text or email, since you consented. Use the phone script.

Cost and obligation

The form is free and hiring is optional. Quote a company, pass on it, or ignore it.

What a good first call sounds like

The person on the line should state their company’s legal name and permit number without being asked, offer to survey your belongings, and explain how the price is built. If they skip all of that and jump to a total, steer them back with question one of the script.

What if none of the three suits you?

Say no, politely. You can submit again later with different details, such as a flexible date, which often changes what a company can offer. There is no penalty for passing.

What if a mover behaves badly?

Write to us at the address below with what happened. We cannot settle your dispute, but we take it seriously when deciding who receives requests. For a Colorado-only move you can also complain to the Public Utilities Commission, and for interstate jobs to the federal motor carrier regulator.

What we ask you to bring

A rough inventory, the access details at both ends, and a date range. The sharper your description, the closer the written quotes will be to the final bill.

Ending contact

Tell the caller to stop, reply STOP to texts, or email zach@eyetoad.com to have your request removed.
