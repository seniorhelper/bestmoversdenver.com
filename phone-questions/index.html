The phone script: ten questions for every mover

Print it or keep it open. Write the answers down. The same ten questions to each company make quotes comparable.

  1. What is your Colorado PUC permit number, and your USDOT number if I am leaving the state?
  2. What is the legal name on that permit, and what is your street address?
  3. Can someone survey my belongings in person or by video before you quote?
  4. Is the price hourly, by weight, or a flat figure? What is the minimum?
  5. When does the clock start and stop, and is travel between sites charged?
  6. What is not included: stairs, long carries, packing materials, heavy items, fuel?
  7. Which payment types do you accept, and when is payment due?
  8. How much liability does your cargo coverage give per item, and what upgrades exist?
  9. Will the crew that quotes me be the crew that moves me, or do you subcontract?
  10. How do I file a claim, and how long do you take to respond?

How to read the answers

Pause on hedging. “We will figure that out on the day” is an answer you should hear in writing. Ask for the contract by email so you can read it without a crew standing by.

After the calls

Lay the three answers side by side. If one quote is far lower, find the missing line item before you celebrate.
